ExpenseTracker is a modern, role-based expense management platform built for teams. Employees can submit expenses, admins can review and approve them, and everyone gets real-time email notifications — all in one clean dashboard.

About the App

ExpenseTracker was built to solve a common problem in organizations — tracking and approving employee expenses without spreadsheets or email chains. It provides a centralized, digital workflow from submission to approval, with full audit history and analytics.

Who is it for?

Companies and teams that want a simple, digital way to manage employee reimbursements and expense approvals.

How it works

Employees submit expense requests. Admins approve or reject them with remarks. Everyone is notified by email instantly.

Analytics & Reports

Visual dashboards, category-wise breakdowns, employee spending trends, and one-click PDF report exports.

Key Features

  • Submit and track expense requests with file attachments
  • Admin approval workflow with remarks and bulk actions
  • Email notifications via Brevo for every status change
  • OTP-based email verification for secure onboarding
  • Forgot password and reset flow via email OTP
  • Category-wise and employee-wise analytics dashboard
  • Export expense reports as PDF — daily, monthly, or yearly
  • Role-based access: Admin and Employee
  • Mobile-friendly and dark mode supported
