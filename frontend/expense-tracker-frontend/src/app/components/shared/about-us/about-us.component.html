Welcome to StudiousPeeps

Where financial management meets intelligent design.

At StudiousPeeps, we believe that managing finances shouldn't be a hassle. Founded by Shruti Markad and Sahil Mahajan, our company is driven by a shared passion for creating intuitive, efficient, and user-centric software solutions that solve real-world problems.

Our Story

The idea for our flagship Expense Tracker was born out of a simple observation: both individuals and growing teams struggle to keep a clear, real-time pulse on their spending. We wanted to bridge the gap between complex financial software and manual spreadsheet tracking. Combining our technical expertise and dedication to clean design, we built a platform that takes the friction out of expense management.

What We Do

At StudiousPeeps, we specialize in building tools that empower you to take control of your financial data. Our Expense Tracker is designed to adapt to your needs—whether you're an employee logging daily bills or an administrator overseeing organizational budgets. From seamless receipt uploads to real-time status tracking and intuitive dashboards, we've streamlined the entire expense lifecycle.

Our Mission

Our mission is simple: to save you time and provide absolute financial clarity. We are committed to continuous innovation, ensuring that every feature we ship at StudiousPeeps adds genuine value, security, and simplicity to your day-to-day operations.
